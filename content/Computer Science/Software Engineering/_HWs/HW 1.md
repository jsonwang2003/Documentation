## 1. Explore the idea of the 10x developer.  Intuitively this feels right that some folks are much better than others, but try to research if these claims are actually grounded in research in the realm of software or are more vibe.  You might even find the clue of where to look reviewing the slides.  This is a one paragraph max answer.  

Such claim of the idea of a 10x developer is more of a vibe than proven fact. According to the article "Individual Productivity Variation in Software Development", Barry Boehm concluded that developing a program with a team of less than optimal programmers ranked by ability typically requires about 3.5 times as many staff-months as developing a program with a team with better ability. This implies that the productivity difference between good and bad programmers are, on average, differed by a factor of 3.5 instead of 10. Such implication therefore shows the idea of a 10x developer statistically doesn't exist.
