=== Study guide for Quiz 1 ===

--- Summary statistics ---

1. What are location and scale parameters?

2. Given a data set, compute the following:
-- Empirical mean
-- Empirical median
-- Empirical variance
-- Empirical covariance
-- Empirical distribution
 
3. Median vs mean.
-- When is the median preferable to the mean?
-- What optimality properties (as a location parameter) does each of them possess?

4. Given a distribution, compute:
-- Expected value
-- Median
-- Variance (two formulas for this)
-- Standard deviation

5. Given the joint distribution of a pair of random variables:
-- Compute their covariance (two formulas for this) and correlation
-- Determine if they are independent

6. Are independent r.v.'s necessarily uncorrelated? Vice versa?

7. Linearity properties.
-- Use linearity of expectation to compute expected values.
-- When does linearity hold for variance?

--- Clustering ---

1. What are two broad uses of clustering?

2. Given a data set, execute the following clustering algorithms on it:
-- Lloyd's method
-- K-means++
-- Farthest-first traversal
-- Single linkage
-- Complete linkage

3. Lloyd's algorithm:
-- What are some ways to initialize the centers?
-- Does this method return the optimal k-means clustering?
-- How might it be adapted for different distance functions?

4. When is k-means not a good clustering method to use?

5. What distinguishes different linkage algorithms from one another?

--- Distances and similarities ---

1. Compute the L1, L2, L-infty norms of vectors.

2. Definition of a norm:
-- What properties must a norm satisfy?
-- What distance function does it yield?
-- What is the unit ball of a norm?

3. What is a metric, exactly?
-- What properties must it satisfy?
-- Are norm-based distances necessarily metrics?

4. KL divergence.
-- Compute the KL divergence between two given probability distributions.

5. Similarity functions
-- What are cosine similarity and Jaccard similarity?
-- What is the danger in using dot product as a similarity measure?
