> [!abstract] Overview
> **Number Representation & Basic Logic Gates** establishes the physical and mathematical building blocks of digital logic circuits. This submodule covers positional number systems (Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal), two-valued Boolean algebra, algebraic simplification theorems, CMOS transistor switch mechanics (nMOS and pMOS), transmission gates, and transistor-level implementations of universal logic gates (NAND and NOR).

---

## Submodule Notes

- [[Computer Systems/Digital Systems/Number Representation & Basic Logic Gates/Number Systems and Boolean Algebra|Number Systems and Boolean Algebra]]
  Positional number encodings (Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal), primary and derived logic gate truth tables, Boolean axioms, single/multi-variable algebraic theorems, duality principles, and formal proof methods (perfect induction and algebraic derivation).

- [[Computer Systems/Digital Systems/Number Representation & Basic Logic Gates/Transistors & Gates|Transistors & Gates]]
  Electrical foundations ($V, I, R, C$), CMOS transistor switches, Pull-Up (pMOS) and Pull-Down (nMOS) networks, transmission gate mechanics, and transistor-level circuit schematics for universal NAND and NOR gates.
