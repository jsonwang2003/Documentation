> [!abstract] Overview
> Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that focuses on training artificial neural networks to recognize patterns and make complex decisions based on large volumes of data. It enables models to automatically extract hierarchical features from raw data, support generalization across diverse applications, and handle vast amounts of unstructured data efficiently.

---
## The Learning Pipeline

Deep learning architectures leverage multiple layers of abstraction to learn meaningful representations. These systems scale across domains with minimal labeled data through the use of specialized techniques:
- **Transfer Learning:** Fine-tune pre-trained models for new tasks.
- **Self-Supervised Learning:** Learn from raw data without external labels.

| [[Self-Supervised Deep Learning]] | Operates entirely on unlabeled data by generating pseudo-labels from intrinsic data properties for scalable pretraining. |
