> [!abstract] Overview
> Ethical AI and policy development ensure that intelligent systems are designed and deployed with accountability, fairness, and societal alignment. This module provides a foundation for understanding and implementing responsible AI practices across technical and governance domains, guiding how AI agents interact with humans, data, and institutions to help mitigate unintended consequences.
