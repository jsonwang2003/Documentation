> [!INFO]
> Harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a balanced measure.

## How It Works

$$
\text{F1} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}
$$

- Combines [[Precision]] and [[Recall]] into a single score  
- Sensitive to both **false positives** and **false negatives**

## What to Look For

- Ideal for imbalanced datasets  
- Useful when both FP and FN matter  
- A single score to summarize model performance

## Application Models

- [[Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)]]  
- [[Deep Neural Network (DNN)]]  
- [[Transformer]]
