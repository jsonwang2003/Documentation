> [!INFO]
> The study of **matter**, **energy**, **motion**, and **fundamental interactions** &rarr; a foundational framework for **natural laws**, **technological innovation**, and **scientific modeling**

- Combines **empirical observation** with **mathematical formalism**
- Bridges domains from [[Mathematics/index|Mathematics]] and **Engineering** to **[[Computer Science/index|Computer Science]]**
- Evolves through **experimentation**, **theoretical abstraction**, and **computational simulation**

## Purpose

- Uncover **universal principles** governing physical systems
- Enable **prediction**, **control**, and **optimization** of natural and engineered phenomena
- Support **technological advancement** and **scientific exploration**

## Benefits

- Powers innovation in **energy**, **transportation**, **communication**, and **materials**
- Enhances understanding of the **universe**, from subatomic particles to cosmic structures
- Provides rigorous tools for **modeling**, **simulation**, and **design**

## Applications

- **Engineering**
	- Drives design of mechanical, electrical, and thermal systems
- **[[Computer Science/index|Computer Science]]**
	- Supports simulation, graphics, and quantum computing
- **[[Mathematics/index|Mathematics]]**
	- Provides structure for differential equations, linear algebra, and vector calculus

## Challenges

- Requires **mathematical fluency** and **conceptual abstraction**
- Demands integration of **theory**, **experiment**, and **computation**
- Can be **counterintuitive** at quantum and relativistic scales

# Physical Paradigm

## [[Physics/Mechanics/index|Mechanics]]

- Rooted in **Newtonian laws** of motion and force
- Explores **kinematics**, **dynamics**, and **energy conservation**
- Extends to **rigid body motion**, **fluid mechanics**, and **chaos theory**
- Powers everything from **vehicle design** to **planetary motion** and **biomechanics**

## [[Physics/Electricity & Magnetism/index|Electricity & Magnetism]]

- Unified by **Maxwell’s equations** describing electric and magnetic fields
- Covers **electrostatics**, **magnetostatics**, **induction**, and **wave propagation**
- Enables technologies like **motors**, **generators**, **wireless communication**, and **optics**
- Forms the basis for **electrical engineering** and **signal theory**

## [[Physics/Thermodynamics/index|Thermodynamics]]

- Studies **heat**, **work**, and **energy transformations**
- Built on **laws of thermodynamics**, **entropy**, and **statistical mechanics**
- Applies to **engines**, **refrigeration**, **chemical reactions**, and **biological systems**
- Informs **efficiency**, **irreversibility**, and **equilibrium modeling**

## [[Physics/Light Optics/index|Light Optics]]

- Explores **light propagation**, **reflection**, **refraction**, and **diffraction**
- Divided into **geometric optics**, **wave optics**, and **quantum optics**
- Enables **lenses**, **lasers**, **fiber optics**, and **spectroscopy**
- Bridges **electromagnetism**, **quantum theory**, and **engineering design**

## [[Physics/Early Quantum Physics/index|Early Quantum Physics]]

- Describes **probabilistic behavior** of particles at atomic and subatomic scales
- Introduces **wave-particle duality**, **superposition**, and **entanglement**
- Formalized through **Schrödinger’s equation**, **Hilbert spaces**, and **operators**
- Powers **quantum computing**, **semiconductors**, and **particle physics**
